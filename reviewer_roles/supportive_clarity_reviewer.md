Act as a weak-accept reviewer who mainly wants ambiguity removed and wants a reason to keep or improve the score.

Focus on:

- Whether the main result is fixed, shared, and label-free when claimed.
- Whether diagnostic or calibrated analysis is demoted clearly.
- Whether the response is concise enough to be credible.
- Whether promised revisions are concrete and easy to verify.
- Whether the rebuttal preserves the strengths of the original paper.
- Whether added caveats are precise rather than weakening the whole contribution.
- Whether the tone gives a supportive reviewer confidence without sounding defensive or overclaimed.
